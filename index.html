Sums Game vs. AI

How to Play

Choose a number from 1-5. The AI will choose its own number. If the sum is even, you win. If the sum is odd, the AI wins.

The AI has 2 modes you can choose from, adaptive and random.

In adaptive mode, the AI learns patterns and uses a basic machine learning algorithm called Bayesian Updating to learn your patterns and defeat you. In random mode, the AI picks numbers randomly.

You will see that when it is in random mode, it is much easier to win against, whereas in adaptive mode, it is much harder.

There is also a visualiser in the bottom-right, where there are nodes whose color and size respond to the probabilities the AI calculates. To reset the AI and restart, reload the page.
