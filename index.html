Sums Game vs. AI

How to Play

Choose a number from 1-5. The AI picks a number too. If the sum is even, you win. If it's odd, the AI wins.

The AI has two modes, adaptive and random. Random mode is easier to beat more often as the AI just guesses. Adaptive mode uses simple Bayesian updating to learn your habits and counter you.

Change modes by clicking the green button.

The bottom-right visualiser shows the AI's probability nodes changing in real time. Reload the page to reset the AI.
